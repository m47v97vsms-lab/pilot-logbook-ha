Flight events

UTC is the authoritative event time and is used for every duration calculation. Local time is a display conversion using the selected airport’s time zone. You can enter either value; changing one recalculates the other. Stored UTC timestamps are never recalculated from local display values.

If a local time occurs twice during the autumn clock change, choose the first or second occurrence. Nonexistent spring-forward times are rejected.
